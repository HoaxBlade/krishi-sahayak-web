**1. What Information is Collected**
\
\
We may collect various types of information from and about users of our Service, including:
\
\
**1.1 Personal Data:**
\
\
Information that can be used to identify you directly or indirectly, such as:
*   **Contact Information:** Name, email address, phone number, postal address.
*   **Account Information:** Username, password, unique user ID.
*   **Demographic Information:** Age, gender, location (if provided).

*   **Communication Data:** Records of your correspondence with us, including customer support inquiries.
\
\
**1.2 Usage Data:**
\
\
Information about how you access and use the Service, which may include:
*   **Device Information:** IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers.
*   **Log Data:** Pages visited, features used, time spent on the Service, access times and dates, and other diagnostic data.
*   **Location Data:** General location derived from your IP address or, if you grant permission, precise location data from your mobile device.
*   **Interaction Data:** How you interact with the Service, such as clicks, scrolls, and navigation paths.
\
\
**1.3 Technical Data:**
\
\
Information about the technology you use to access our Service, such as internet service provider, mobile network information, and device capabilities.
\
\
\
**2. How Information is Collected**
\
\
We collect information in several ways:
\
\
**2.1 Direct Input:**
You provide information directly to us when you:
*   Register for an account.
*   Fill out forms on the Service (e.g., profile updates, feedback forms).
*   Submit agricultural data or other content.
*   Communicate with us via email, chat, or other channels.
\
\
**2.2 Automated Means:**
\
\
As you navigate through and interact with our Service, we may use automatic data collection technologies to gather certain information about your equipment, browsing actions, and patterns, including:
*   **Cookies and Tracking Technologies:** (See our Cookie Management Policy for details) These help us understand user behavior, improve our Service, and deliver personalized content.
*   **Server Logs:** Our servers automatically record information that your browser sends whenever you visit a website.
*   **Analytics Tools:** We use third-party analytics services (e.g., Google Analytics) to collect and analyze Usage Data.
\
\
**2.3 From Third Parties:**
We may receive information about you from third parties, such as partners, service providers, or publicly available sources, where permitted by law.

**3. How Information is Used**
\
\
We use the information we collect for various purposes, including:

*   **To Provide and Maintain the Service:** To operate, deliver, and improve the functionality of Krishi Sahayak, including displaying agricultural insights, managing user accounts, and processing your requests.
*   **To Personalize Your Experience:** To tailor content, features, and recommendations based on your agricultural data and usage patterns.
*   **To Communicate with You:** To send you important updates, notifications, marketing communications (with your consent where required), and respond to your inquiries.
*   **For Research and Development:** To analyze usage trends, conduct research, and develop new features and services.
*   **For Security and Fraud Prevention:** To protect the security and integrity of our Service, detect and prevent fraudulent activities, and enforce our Terms & Conditions.
*   **For Legal Compliance:** To comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal processes, and governmental requests.
*   **For Aggregated and Anonymized Data:** To create aggregated and anonymized statistical data for research, analysis, and reporting, which does not identify you personally.

\
\
We may share your information in the following circumstances:

*   **With Service Providers:** We may share your information with third-party vendors, consultants, and other service providers who perform services on our behalf (e.g., hosting, data analysis, customer support, marketing). These service providers are obligated to protect your information and use it only for the purposes for which it was disclosed.
*   **With Business Partners:** We may share information with trusted business partners for joint offerings or promotions, with your consent where required.
*   **For Legal Reasons:** We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., a court order or government agency).
*   **In Case of Business Transfer:** In connection with, or during negotiations of, any merger, sale of company assets, financing, or acquisition of all or a portion of our business by another company.
*   **With Your Consent:** We may share your information with other third parties when we have your explicit consent to do so.
*   **Aggregated or Anonymized Data:** We may share aggregated or anonymized data that cannot reasonably be used to identify you with third parties for various purposes, including research, analytics, and marketing.

\

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including for the purposes of satisfying any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. To determine the appropriate retention period, we consider the amount, nature, and sensitivity of the personal data, the potential risk of harm from unauthorized use or disclosure of your personal data, the purposes for which we process your personal data, and whether we can achieve those purposes through other means, and the applicable legal requirements.

**6. User Rights**
\
\
Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

*   **Right to Access:** Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
*   **Right to Rectification:** Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
*   **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** Request the deletion of your personal data under certain circumstances.
*   **Right to Restrict Processing:** Request that we limit the processing of your personal data under certain conditions.
*   **Right to Data Portability:** Request to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.
*   **Right to Object:** Object to the processing of your personal data under certain circumstances, including for direct marketing.
*   **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Withdraw your consent at any time where we rely on consent to process your personal data.
\
\
To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the contact information provided below. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling your request.
